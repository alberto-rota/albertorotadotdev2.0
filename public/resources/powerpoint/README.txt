Place your PowerPoint at:

  public/resources/powerpoint/deck.pptx

Export your slides as images and put them here (matching products.json):

  public/resources/powerpoint/slides/slide-01.svg
  public/resources/powerpoint/slides/slide-02.svg
  ...

Tip: for best quality/performance on the web, export to WEBP (or PNG) and update:
  - src/data/products.json -> viewer.slides.ext
  - src/data/products.json -> viewer.slides.count
